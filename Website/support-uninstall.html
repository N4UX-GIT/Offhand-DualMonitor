Help center › Dual-monitor setup

Return to one monitor or uninstall Offhand

You can play on one screen again and keep Offhand for later. If you’re removing the addon, restore your usual window and HUD while you can still open its settings.

Keep Offhand for laterTurn off spanning and return to your usual UI. Remove OffhandRestore your UI, then uninstall from the WoW client you play.

Return WoW to one monitor

  1. Undo the span. Use the method you chose at setup.
    • Companion: In Advanced, turn off Auto-span WoW on launch. With WoW open, click Restore Window, then Exit Companion. Restore Window pauses auto-span for this session. Turning off auto-span keeps it off on the next launch. If the old window size is unavailable, move and resize WoW on your game monitor.
    • FancyZones: Drag WoW by its title bar out of the combined zone without holding Shift. Move it to your usual monitor and resize or maximize it. You can keep the zone for later.
    • Manual placement or macOS: In Windowed mode, drag WoW onto your usual display and resize it. If you used a split on one ultrawide display, leave the window at the size you want to play in.
  2. Turn off the Offhand split. In the game world, type /offhand. Under Display & HUD, clear Enable Offhand Dual Monitor Mode. In WoW’s graphics settings, select your usual display mode. If you already removed the addon, skip its checkbox.
  3. Bring back your HUD. Outside combat, open Blizzard Edit Mode if your client offers it. Select the layout you used before Offhand. If another addon manages your bars, frames or chat, select your previous profile in that addon. If text or buttons look the wrong size, search WoW’s settings for UI Scale and set it to your preferred value.

A panel is still missing? With Offhand installed, open /offhand and use Recover Missing UI for off-screen elements. If your original HUD layout or profile was overwritten, use your pre-setup backup if you have one, or choose an available layout and adjust it on one screen.

Keeping Offhand? Go straight to the relaunch check. When you want to span again, re-enable dual-monitor mode and reapply your usual placement method.

Remove Offhand

  1. Restore your window and HUD first. Follow the one-monitor steps above. Once the game looks right, exit WoW normally so it saves your settings.
  2. Remove the addon from the correct client. In CurseForge or WowUp, select the WoW installation you play, find Offhand and uninstall it. For a manual installation, move just the Offhand folder out of that client’s Interface/AddOns directory. Keep your WTF folder, other addons and any Offhand_UserMedia artwork you want to reuse.
  3. Remove Companion if you also want it gone. For the Microsoft Store app, choose Exit Companion, open Windows Start, find Offhand Companion, right-click it and choose Uninstall. For a portable copy, exit the app and remove its extracted program folder after saving any files you want to keep. Windows uninstall help.

Check your next launch

Fully exit WoW and start the same client again. Enter the world on the same character. Check that WoW stays on your chosen screen, your action bars and chat are in place, text is the size you expect, and your map and bags open within reach.

If WoW spans again, check Companion’s auto-span setting or the zone the window opened in. If only the HUD looks different, check the active Edit Mode layout or UI addon profile for that character. Get help with your setup.
